v0.9.7
A big touch-and-remote release that fixes everything found in the 0.9.6
review (docs/audit-0.9.6.md). It was checked against Kodi's source and in
preview renders only, not yet on the Pi. Please test it on the box, both
by finger and by remote.

Playing video, music and games
- The on-screen controls are now one bar along the bottom edge instead of
  a box in the middle of the picture. Tap the picture above the bar to hide
  it. "Hide" only hides the bar. NEW "Menus" takes you back to the browser
  or desktop while the film, song or game keeps playing; Now playing on the
  task bar brings you back.
- Volume by touch: a mute button (a speaker, crossed out while muted) and a
  volume slider on the bar and in the mini player. You can also tap the
  speaker/volume gauge at the top right of the menu bar to open the mini
  player.
- Seek by touch: drag the position bar or tap anywhere on it. On live TV it
  seeks within the timeshift buffer.
- Rewind and Fast-forward now really rewind and fast-forward (they used to
  pause). Play turns into Pause while something plays.
- The buttons are bigger and drawn in the palette's text colour, so they
  are readable on dark palettes too. A tap in the small gap between two
  buttons hits the nearer one.
- New buttons: Info (with a progress bar, plot and Process info), Playlist
  (what is queued), Subtitles, Audio, Video, Bookmarks and Add-on settings.
  Add-on settings now works however playback started (from the add-on, a
  favourite or a desktop tile).
- When you pause, skip or fast-forward, a thin strip at the bottom shows
  the position and the speed or "Paused". Buffering shows a box with the
  percentage. Zoom/view-mode changes show full width.
- Bookmarks opens as a panel at the bottom, so the video stays visible. Its
  buttons have labels, and Clear asks first (it also deletes the resume
  point).
- Music: shuffle and repeat show whether they are on. NEW Visualisation
  button, plus Preset and Locked when the visualisation has presets.
  Without a visualisation add-on, full-screen music shows the fanart or a
  big cover instead of black. Radio streams no longer show a stray " - ".
- Games: Controls, Ports and Players in the game menu, and a note that only
  controllers can play.
- Remote: opening the bar starts on Play/Pause, so OK can't repeat Stop by
  accident. Left/Right go round both rows.

Live TV and radio
- NEW real TV/Radio guide grid: channels down the left, a time ruler along
  the top, a "now" line, and red dots for recordings. The strip on the left
  has Group, Now, -12 h, +12 h, Date, Page up/down, Information, Options
  and Navigate... Tap a programme to select it, long-press it for the menu,
  drag to scroll. The guide is titled "Guide" (it said "Channel").
- The guide's Navigate... box was six blank buttons. It now has labelled
  jumps and sits on the bottom edge with the grid in view.
- On the bar while watching: Channels, Guide, Teletext, Record (which reads
  Stop recording while recording), and Radio text for radio. A swipe up or
  down previews another channel: its name and programme show at the top and
  the bar offers "Switch to channel". Previous/Next always come as a pair.
- The channel list while watching shows number, logo, what is on now with
  times and progress, now/next for the highlighted channel, and arrows to
  change group. Tap outside it to close.
- Programme and recording info show channel, date, start-end time, episode
  and genre. The five guide-only buttons on recordings, which did nothing,
  are gone.
- Channels: Group and Show hidden channels buttons. Recordings: Group
  items, Deleted recordings and the watched filter. Timers: Hide disabled.
  The channel manager, group manager and guide search have a caption on
  every button.

Pictures
- Pictures and slideshows really show the picture (a grey sheet covered
  it).
- Tap the picture for a strip with Previous, Play/Pause, Next, Info and
  Close. The strip hides itself after 5 seconds.
- Pictures gets View slideshow, Recursive slideshow and Randomise buttons.
  Picture info shows the picture beside its details.

Touch
- NEW Power button on the task bar (shut down, reboot, exit). Power is also
  in the Settings hub and the desktop's Settings tile, for when the task
  bar is hidden.
- In Touch mode every pop-up has its own Back button at the bottom right
  (the task bar doesn't react while a pop-up is open). With "Hide the task
  bar" on, a Back button still floats at the bottom right.
- Finger-sized in Touch mode: the title-bar boxes (wider and
  spaced apart, with × alone at the far right), task-bar buttons (the whole
  bar height reacts), the browser toolbars, dialog button rows, info-dialog
  buttons, the select lists' OK/Cancel row, spin arrows, slider knobs,
  scrollbars and the picture picker rows.
- The on-screen keyboard gets 56 px keys in Touch mode on the 7" panel, and
  on the 4:3 screen.
- Things that couldn't be scrolled by finger now can: the text viewer
  (changelogs, logs), long confirmations, plots and add-on descriptions,
  the privacy policy, the skin settings list and its help, and the tile
  catalogue.
- The context menu can be twice as tall, with a scrollbar when needed. A
  tap outside closes it; a tap that just misses an entry no longer does.
- Tapping the dimmed background closes the OSD settings, the Power menu
  and the mini player. It never closes the keyboard, number pad,
  confirmations or add-on settings, so nothing you typed is lost.
- Toolbar "Options" opens the menu for the selected item (it did nothing).
- Skin settings > NEW "Pointer in Touch mode": Auto / Always / Never. In
  Auto, a touchscreen's own built-in "mouse" no longer keeps the pointer
  on. Mouse detection also starts when Kodi starts in a window other than
  the desktop.
- Screen calibration: the markers are visible, and the hint says what
  works by touch (tap to pick the next marker, swipe to nudge it, tap and
  hold or Back to finish).

Remote and keyboard
- The on-screen keyboard and the number pad work with a remote: Up and Down
  move between the rows (before, only one row could be reached).
- Select lists: Up from OK/Cancel goes back to the list. Info screens
  always open with a button selected. Nothing gets stuck on the touch Back
  buttons or the scrollbars.

Missing text and dead buttons
- The number pad shows 0-9 again, not "Programs", "Pictures"...
- The on-screen keyboard's title says what it is asking for (Wi-Fi
  password, search, name...).
- Add/Edit media source: Browse, Add, Remove and Name have captions, and a
  scrollbar shows when there are many paths.
- Audio/subtitle delay and similar sliders: the title names the setting,
  and - and + step the value.
- Every slider now shows its bar and knob (they were invisible).
- Settings: the Basic/Standard/Advanced/Expert button works, and add-on
  settings have one too.
- Pop-ups that had an empty title bar (player, power menu, bookmarks, PVR
  dialogs, info) now have a title.
- Smart playlist editor and rule, music playlist editor (you can see the
  playlist you are building), video versions manager, event log, profiles
  and colour picker (now with swatches): every button is labelled and the
  dead blank ones are gone.
- Power menu: "Enter/Leave master mode" instead of "Lock settings". The task
  bar's Start follows Kodi's language.
- Background jobs (library scan, guide import) show a small box in the
  bottom-right corner instead of dimming the whole screen. The Busy box has
  a Cancel you can tap.

Browsers and info
- Library, playlist and add-on windows have a second row of buttons for
  that window: play queue Shuffle/Repeat/Save/Clear; Videos and Music
  Search, Watched filter, Party mode and Update library; inside add-ons,
  Refresh and Add-on start page.
- Lists, posters and icons show a tick when watched, a half circle when
  part-watched, and a play mark on what is playing.
- Posters and icons show the real picture (episode thumbs, add-on art, file
  and folder icons), and their titles wrap onto a second line.
- Album and artist info show the cover. Film/TV info hides Trailer,
  Director, Versions and Extras when there is nothing behind them, and Play
  reads Browse on a TV show or season.
- Sort Order says Ascending or Descending, and View says List, Poster or
  Icons. Empty folders say "Empty list". The item count also shows inside
  add-ons.
- The current value in every settings choice list is readable when
  highlighted (it was dark red on blue). Chosen rows get a tick.

Desktop and skin settings
- "Defaults" is now "Reset skin settings..." and asks first, offering
  Reset, Back up and Cancel. Touch mode and the pointer choice survive a
  reset.
- Back up / Restore: Cancel really cancels. Restore no longer brings back
  an old parked-window button.
- Tiles: Rename is greyed out on an empty slot, so "(empty)" tiles no
  longer appear. Renaming a library tile starts empty, so the tile keeps
  following its own label.
- "Scroll list labels" is now "Scroll long descriptions", and it really
  decides whether plots and descriptions scroll by themselves.
- "Hide the date and clock" also hides the task-bar clock. Minimise (_) no
  longer throws away the window you had parked before.
- Customise desktop: Done is no longer cut off in Touch mode / Extra large.
  The tile catalogue lists can be scrolled. The icon picker uses its whole
  box and no longer offers the small status marks.

4:3 screen (1024x768)
- The keyboard, the context menu and the Settings hub use the extra height,
  with sharper icons. System information and the weather no longer leave
  big gaps. The icon picker shows two more rows.

Fonts
- The bitmap font now has curly quotes, dashes, the ellipsis, bullet, euro
  and trademark signs, so YouTube and Plex titles no longer lose them.
- NEW font choice for other alphabets (Polish, Greek, Russian, Japanese...):
  Settings > Interface > Skin > Fonts > "Unicode (all alphabets)". It uses
  Kodi's own smooth font at the same sizes. Kodi doesn't switch to it by
  itself, so choose it when you need it.

Install and Network & Bluetooth
- install-arthuros.sh switches the skin on in Kodi by itself, so it shows up
  in the skin list on a fresh box. It works on LibreELEC (root, no sudo),
  and never leaves Kodi stopped or without a skin if a step fails. It can
  add the polkit rule that lets Network & Bluetooth change Wi-Fi when Kodi
  runs as a service; it asks once.
- Wi-Fi (netbt.py): if a saved open network fails to connect, you now get a
  message saying why. "Not allowed to manage networks" names the user Kodi
  really runs as.

v0.9.5
- NEW 4:3 layout, 1024x768, for screens such as an iPad 3 panel
  (2048x1536). Kodi picks it by itself on a 4:3 screen and draws it at
  exactly 2x, so the pixel font and icons stay sharp. 16:9 screens keep the

  - Every window uses the extra height: lists, panels, text and the desktop
    show more rows; the task bar stays at the bottom.
  - Desktop tiles are taller, with bigger icons (96 px on the 3x3 grid, 64 px

  - Dialogs keep their size and stay centred.

v0.9.4
- NEW Network & Bluetooth, for Kodi on plain Linux such as Raspberry Pi OS,
  where LibreELEC's own settings aren't available. It's in Settings, in the
  Settings menu on the desktop, and in the Everything menu. It appears only
  when LibreELEC's settings add-on isn't installed.
  - Wi-Fi: nearby networks with signal bars, join with a password, hidden
    networks, forget, disconnect, Wi-Fi on/off.
  - Bluetooth: paired devices with connect, disconnect and forget; pair a new
    device (scan, pair, trust and connect in one go, with passkeys shown on
    screen).
  - Audio output: pick Kodi's sound device. After a speaker or headphones
    connect, it offers to switch to them.
  Uses NetworkManager (nmcli) and BlueZ (bluetoothctl), which Raspberry Pi
  OS already has. It can also be put on a desktop tile: Customise desktop ->
  Choose what it opens -> Settings -> Network & Bluetooth.

v0.9.3
- NEW Touch mode (Skin settings), for touchscreens:
  - every list, menu and settings row is taller, so each is an easy finger
    target (the same row heights the Extra large fonts use, with normal-size
    text);
  - a Back button on the task bar, full task-bar height, where the clock sat
    (the menu bar still shows the time);
  - no mouse pointer. Kodi shows the pointer after every tap; Touch mode hides

  Turning it on or off reloads the skin.

v0.9.2
- NEW "Everything" menu for the desktop: every top-level menu Estuary and
  Confluence offer, in one list. Movies, TV shows, music videos, the video
  and music libraries, files and playlists, pictures, TV channels, guide,
  recordings, timers, radio, games, the add-on browser, programs, favorites,

- Add-ons menu: the add-on browser itself, and Game add-ons.
- Settings menu: All settings, Services, TV, Games, Profiles, Events and

v0.9.1
- FIXED Customise desktop showing the wrong icons. Choosing an icon didn't
  change the picture in the editor, and moving a tile showed the icon of
  whatever tile first lived in that place. Kodi treats a list-item property
  called "icon" as the item's picture and never updates it; the skin now uses
  a different name. The desktop itself was always right.
- FIXED the tile numbers in Customise desktop showing words ("Mu...",
  "Vi..."). Kodi reads a bare number in a list item as a string id, so tile
  3 showed string 3, "Videos". The numbers now show as numbers.
- Tile names now wrap onto two or three lines instead of ending in "...".
  Icons are a size smaller to make room: 80 pixels on 3-row grids, 48 on 4
  rows, 32 on 5 and 6 rows. They are still drawn at exact pixel multiples.
- The Tile dialog's preview wraps long names too.
- NEW ArthurOS-style icons for add-on tiles: online video, media server,
  game launcher, radio and system tools. An add-on tile gets one
  automatically, picked from the kind of add-on it is: YouTube gets online
  video, Plex, Jellyfin and Emby get media server, and so on. The icons are
  also in Choose an icon...
- NEW "Add-on's own picture" in the Tile dialog, to use the add-on's own
  artwork instead.
- NEW Back up skin settings... and Restore skin settings... in Skin settings.
  A backup is one small file holding the desktop, grid size, pointer, bar and
  clock choices, plus the skin's colors, fonts and zoom. Restore asks first,
  changes only what differs, and reloads the desktop.

v0.9.0

  - Grid size: 3x3, 4x3, 4x4, 5x4, 5x5, 6x5 or 6x6. The panel on the right

v0.8.0
- FIXED the spin arrows being cut off on the right of settings rows (Wait
  time and others). Kodi measures spinposx in from the right edge; the skin
  had -6, which pushed the up arrow past the edge. validate.py now rejects
  negative values.
- FIXED empty game controller setup. DialogGameControllers.xml is shared by
  three windows (controllers, ports, players), each with its own controls;
  it now picks the right layout the way Estuary does, and the controller
  list, button list, controller picture and Help/Reset/Get more buttons are
  where Kodi expects them.
- FIXED blank add-on descriptions. Kodi 21 exposes add-on details as
  ListItem.Addon* labels; the skin used names from older Kodi that are always
  empty. The add-on window now shows version, author, summary, origin, size
  and the full description, and the add-on browser's side panel shows them too.
- FIXED missing buttons across less-used windows. The tool that reads which
  controls a window needs skipped any id passed in through an include, so,
  for example, the video info screen had no Play, Resume or Trailer, the
  playlist editors had no OK or Cancel, and the version manager and bookmarks
  had no buttons. The video, music and TV info screens now carry all of Kodi's
  buttons; subtitle search has its services list back.
- New fonts: Large bold, Extra large and Extra large bold. Extra large is the
  bitmap font at exactly twice its size (still crisp), and lists, settings
  and menus switch to taller rows while it is active.
- New colors: Colorblind-Safe and Colorblind-Safe-Dark (Okabe-Ito blue and
  vermillion), High-Contrast-Light and High-Contrast-Dark (7:1 on every
  pairing). Every palette now meets 4.5:1; palettecheck.py measures it.
- My add-ons icon carries a heart, so it no longer depends on orange vs green.
- US English: the skin's own words have US spellings when Kodi's language is
  English (US), matching Kodi's own (Colors, Favorites).
- Weather and Favorites tiles open directly, as they hold only one entry.
- Chrome text (menu bar, task bar, title bars) has its own font names, so
  bigger fontsets no longer overflow the bars.

v0.7.2
- FIXED the weather flashing a second, raw forecast (Celsius shown as
  "27 °C°F", lower-case text, ISO dates, placeholder icons) whenever it
  refreshed. The weather add-on writes its raw values into the same window
  properties Kodi later overwrites with converted ones, and the skin was
  reading those properties. It now uses the labels Kodi fills from its own
  converted data, which only change once a refresh has finished.
- Weather: a Refresh button, and a Next location button that appears only
  when more than one location is set up. The location name and last update
  time moved to the bottom panel.
- FIXED the Options (context) menu opening half off the right edge with its
  dimmed backdrop shifted across the screen. Kodi places this menu itself by
  moving the whole window next to the focused list; the skin had centred the
  menu inside the window as well, so it was pushed off by the same amount.
  validate.py now checks for this.
- FIXED a file-manager item losing its name after you open Options on it.
  Kodi marks that item, and draws marked items in the label's "selected"
  colour, which defaults to fully transparent. Marked items now show in a
  palette colour (red on the light palettes).

v0.7.1
- FIXED the keyboard stopping at "Scroll list labels" in Skin settings.
  Kodi chains a grouplist's Up/Down by control id, and the "Pointer" heading
  had no id (id 0 means "nowhere"), so Down went no further. The same fault
  was hiding in the player controls and the video, music and game OSDs: the
  Playlists-style icon button, Subtitles and Close were unreachable by
  keyboard. validate.py now rejects any id-less control inside a grouplist.
- FIXED the _ / fullscreen / x boxes turning into dark squares in Amber,
  Phosphor and Midnight. The whole box, glyph included, was tinted by the
  palette's face colour, so a black glyph stayed black on a dark face. The
  face and glyph are now separate: the face follows the palette, the glyph
  uses the palette's text colour, and pointing at a box fills it with the
  selection colour. Greyed-out boxes use a stippled glyph so they still show.

v0.7.0
- FIXED the "Please wait" start-up screen that never went away. Kodi counts
  Startup.xml as part of its own start-up and waits for the skin to hand over
  to the Home screen. ArthurOS never did, so Kodi sat there: the UI was never
  marked ready, colour and font changes were silently refused, the remote
  (CEC) waited, and the window ignores the mouse by design - which is why its
  _ and x boxes did nothing. Pressing Esc left Kodi on an empty screen, where
  the pointer trails came from. Startup now replaces itself immediately, like
  Estuary's. validate.py rejects a Startup.xml without the hand-over.
- Title-bar boxes, rethought:
  _ on a window parks it on the task bar and shows the desktop. The task-bar
    button brings it back to the folder it was showing.
  x on a window closes it and returns to the desktop.
  On a dialog, _ closes every dialog and x closes that one.
  The fullscreen box is greyed out when nothing is playing, and on the
  desktop's own panel _ and x are greyed out, since the desktop cannot close.
  Busy, progress, seek and notification panels have no boxes at all, and
  the login screen cannot be minimised past.
- Every icon is now unique: 177 drawings from one registry (iconset.py),
  instead of 125 Kodi fallbacks shared among 25 pictures. Related icons share
  a picture plus a corner badge: Movies, Recently added movies and Movie
  genres are a film strip with nothing, a star, and a tag. New art for
  Videos, Player, Media, Interface, Skin, Services, System, Games, Live TV,
  Profiles, Events, albums, the power menu, warnings and errors.
  iconcheck.py fails the build if two different icons share pixels, if two
  Kodi fallbacks share an icon without being declared synonyms, or if any
  submenu or tile panel repeats an icon.
- Every window's title bar shows its own icon; Settings categories and the
  desktop panel follow what they are showing.
- The now-playing task-bar button is unchanged.

v0.4.0
- Enter (or a click) on a home icon now moves focus into the context panel on
  the right instead of opening the window straight away, so you scroll the
  sub-menu and pick from there. SetFocus(9010,0) also snaps to the first row,

v0.3.1
- THE CURSOR FIX FINALLY SHIPS. Pointer.xml is generated by genwindows.py, and
  the id fix had been applied by hand to the generated file twice, then wiped
  both times by the next generator run. v0.2.0 and v0.3.0 both went out with
  the original id-less Pointer.xml, which is why there was never a cursor.
  Fixed in the generator, checked by validate.py, and covered by audit.sh.
- Removed <itemlayout>/<focusedlayout> from Defaults.xml. Kodi's SetDefaults
  appends every child of a <default> block unconditionally, so those were
  landing on top of the layout each list already had. The reference skin
  defines no container defaults at all.
- Added a "Large" fontset, so Settings > Interface > Skin > Fonts is a real
  choice (16px -> 20px body text) instead of one row.
- validate.py now checks: Pointer has cursor ids 1-4, fontset idloc resolves

  off-screen. All eight are now real buttons.
- FIXED ActivateWindow(Favourites), which is not a window name Kodi knows.
  It is "favouritesbrowser". Every ActivateWindow target is now checked
  against Kodi's own table by validate.py.
- FIXED DialogBusy referring to Window(Busy); the name is "busydialog".
